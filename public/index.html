A multi-method workflow

Combine methods instead of choosing one.

A typical study moves between methods — and back again. Here is how each step works today, and how data gets from one to the next.

  1. 01

    Collect

    Gather discussions from the subreddits, searches and threads where your population talks.

    Add to project sends posts, comments and engagement into coding.
  2. 02

    Code

    AI applies your codebook; Coder 1 and Coder 2 code a sample independently; reliability shows where they agree.

    Adjudication records final codes without overwriting anyone's.
  3. 03

    Map

    Find central actors, brokers and communities in who replies to whom.

    Reply network export from the scraper opens in Network Analysis.
  4. 04

    Survey

    Ask the same population targeted questions and collect original responses.

    Add text answers to project brings open answers into coding.
  5. 05

    Synthesize

    Compare themes, engagement and responses across sources for a fuller picture.

    Metrics and exports: engagement per code, CSV and JSON for your analysis.

Use the steps in any order, or only the ones you need. The connections above are the ones the platform supports directly; anything else moves between methods as standard CSV or JSON.

Research integrity

Built for research you can defend.

Rigor and participant protection are part of how the platform works, not an afterthought.

  • Transparent AI

    Every AI code shows its confidence and reason, and AI results stay separate from human coding — they never overwrite a coder's decision.

  • Measured reliability

    Two independent human coders, standard agreement statistics with their inputs stated, and adjudication kept apart from the original decisions.

  • Privacy by default

    Projects stay in your browser. Surveys are anonymous unless you add identification, and session timelines are recorded only when respondents are told first.

  • Platform rules respected

    Data collection goes through Reddit's API, RedditAPIs.com or ordinary page requests, keeps to rate limits, and never logs in, solves CAPTCHAs or gets around blocks.
